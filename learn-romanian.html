Words

Learn Romanian

Seven short lessons. Standard spelling, with diacritics. Not a course, and not a promise that you will be fluent.

These sit next to the word list and the stories. Each page has examples in Romanian and English, then a tiny practice. The translator uses the same stock of words. Regional notes, when they appear, are the honest few — pâine heard as pâne, and the rest on dialects.

  1. 1. GreetingsThe hellos you actually say.
  2. 2. Numbers and familyOne, two, and the people in the house.
  3. 3. Food and the tableBread, water, please, the bill.
  4. 4. House and directionsRooms, left and right, where is.
  5. 5. Verbs in the presentSunt, am, merg, vreau, nu înțeleg.
  6. 6. Travel and askingTickets, the station, slower please.
  7. 7. Polite formsVă rog, dumneavoastră, scuzați.
