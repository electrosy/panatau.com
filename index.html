History

Dacia, then Rome

The mountains were a kingdom before they were a province. Trajan’s campaigns left roads, forts, and a Latin that never fully left the language.

Principalities

Wallachia, Moldavia, and Transylvania spent centuries between empires. The painted churches and wooden gates are what that pressure left standing.

Union, then the century

The modern state is young next to the ridges. What the pictures hold is older than any one border: church, gate, street, high water.

Recipes

Four common ones, kept simple. Home versions, not a canon.

Mămăligă

Serves 4

  • 1 cup coarse cornmeal
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • A spoon of butter, if you want it softer

Bring the water and salt to a boil. Rain in the cornmeal while you stir so it does not clump. Lower the heat and stir often until it pulls from the side of the pot, about 20 minutes. Rest 5 minutes. Slice with a wet knife or a string. Eat with cheese, eggs, or mujdei.

Mujdei

A small bowl

  • 4 garlic cloves
  • A pinch of salt
  • 3 tablespoons oil, or the same of water
  • A squeeze of lemon, optional

Crush the garlic with the salt until it is a paste. Stir in the oil a little at a time, or use water for a sharper sauce. Lemon if you like it bright. It should be loose enough to spoon, not a mayonnaise. Good on potatoes, grilled meat, or mămăligă.

Salată de varză

Serves 4

  • Half a small white cabbage, sliced thin
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar
  • A little dill, if you have it

Toss the cabbage with the salt and leave it 10 minutes. Squeeze out the extra water. Stir in oil, vinegar, and dill. Taste. It should be sharp and a bit wet, not a dry slaw. Better after a short rest in the cold.

Ouă cu telemea

Serves 2

  • 3 eggs
  • A handful of telemea, or any salty white cheese
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon butter or oil
  • Salt only if the cheese is mild

Warm the fat in a small pan. Add the onions for a minute. Crumble in the cheese, then pour in the beaten eggs. Cook low, folding once, until just set. Telemea is already salty, so taste before you add salt.
