Romania

Pictures, not a catalog.

History

A few rooms, not a textbook.

Dacia, then Rome

The mountains were a kingdom before they were a province. Trajan’s campaigns left roads, forts, and a Latin that never fully left the language.

Principalities

Wallachia, Moldavia, and Transylvania spent centuries between empires. The painted churches and wooden gates are what that pressure left standing.

Union, then the century

The modern state is young next to the ridges. What the pictures hold is older than any one border: church, gate, street, high water.

Store

A table, not a checkout. Nothing here is for sale yet.

Painted cup

Blue flowers on cream. The kind of cup that stays on the table after the tea is gone.

Embroidered cloth

A short scarf with a folk band. Fringe, a little wear, made to be used.

Brass key

Not a product. A placeholder, until there is a real object and a real price.

Maps

An imagined atlas plate. Not for navigation.

The arc

The Carpathians bend through the middle. Almost every picture on this site sits on one side of that arc or the other.

The river

The Danube is the southern edge, then the delta. The sea is a small blue corner on the plate.

How to read it

No city names on purpose. It is dressing for the place, not a route.
