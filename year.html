The year

How the calendar sits on the table, in the church, and in the handwork. Fixed days are given as the country keeps them now. Easter moves.

Winter

The year in the house starts at Christmas, not in the ledger. December 25 is Christmas. The fast before it is the harder half of the month. January 1 is Saint Basil, and the new year is eaten as much as counted. January 6 is Boboteaza, the blessing of water. In villages the priest still walks to wells and doors. The food of this stretch is pork if a pig was killed, cabbage, bread, and what was put up in autumn. Mămăligă does not care what month it is.

Late winter, then the first flower

February 24 is Dragobete, a day for pairing and for the first birds. It is not Valentine’s Day with a different name, though towns now sell it that way. March 1 is Mărțișor: a red and white thread, pinned on, worn until a tree flowers. The thread is small. The point is that winter is being argued with.

Spring and the moving feast

Easter is the high hinge. The date moves with the moon, and it does not always match the Western date. Before it, the long fast. Eggs are dyed, and in Bucovina drawn with wax. Lamb or a simpler soup, cozonac if the house can manage the dough, and the greeting Hristos a înviat. April 23 is Saint George, when the year outside the house is said to open. After Easter the table loosens. Green onion, radish, and fresh cheese.

Summer

Work is outside. The church year is quieter except for village feasts, hramuri, when a church keeps its own saint and the village eats in the yard. Tomatoes are worth eating. The sea at Constanța is a summer road, not a winter one. August 15 is the Dormition of Mary, a major feast, and a day a lot of people still travel to a monastery even if they do not go the rest of the year.

Autumn

October 14 is Saint Parascheva. Iași fills. October 26 is Saint Demetrius, the other hinge, when animals come down from the high grass and the year turns toward the pot. November 30 is Saint Andrew, kept as a patron of the country. Then the Christmas fast begins again in the middle of November, and the cloth work comes back inside.

Nothing on this page is a full ritual. It is the order of the year, so the recipes, the eggs, and the saints have a season to stand in.
