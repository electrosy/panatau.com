The dictionary works without a network. Type a word from the list, or a short phrase.

Seeded from the site word list, plus greetings and travel phrases. Reverse lookup is built in. It is not a full dictionary, and it will not invent grammar it does not have. Longer leftover sentences may try a free public fallback (MyMemory). If that fails, the dictionary still stands.

A few entries carry regional notes — pâine heard as pâne, a merge beside a umbla in Moldavia, and others we are sure of. The dialect layer is partial on purpose. Background on dialects.
