7. Polite forms

Please, sorry, and the you that keeps distance.

Vă rog is please to a stranger, a shop, an elder, or more than one person. Te rog is the same to someone you know. Both sit at the end of a request more often than at the start: o cafea, vă rog. Thank you is mulțumesc; add mult for many thanks. The reply is cu plăcere, with pleasure, or nu-i nimic.

Excuse me: scuzați in a crowd or to interrupt. Scuză-mă to a friend. Sorry for a real fault is îmi pare rău.

Dumneavoastră takes plural verb forms even for one person: dumneavoastră sunteți, aveți, mergeți. That is the polite machinery. You do not mix tu with vă in the same address. Sărut mâna is still used to an older woman, literally I kiss your hand, as a hello. You can answer with a nod and bună ziua if you are not from here. Poftiți means come in, or here you are when something is handed over.

  • vă rog Please, polite.
  • te rog Please, to someone you know.
  • mulțumesc Thank you.
  • cu plăcere You're welcome.
  • scuzați Excuse me, to a stranger.
  • îmi pare rău I am sorry.
  • dumneavoastră You, formal. Takes plural verbs.
  • sărut mâna Greeting to an older woman.
  • poftiți Come in, or here you are.
  • bine ați venit Welcome, formal.

Practice

Dumneavoastră looks plural because the verb is plural. The person may still be one.

That is the set. Keep them on words, read them in stories, or check a phrase in the translator. English from the Romanian side: learn English.
