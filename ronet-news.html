2 September 2026 · Agerpres

Harghita, first green destination in the country

Harghita received the Green Destinations Silver Award and is the first Romanian county on that map. The audit scored culture and traditions highest. The county is also lined up as a European Region of Gastronomy for 2027.

3 September 2026 · Agerpres

First European research grant for Romanian psychology

Babeș-Bolyai University in Cluj won an ERC Starting Grant, about 1.5 million euro, for a five-year study of how unconscious learning becomes conscious experience. Agerpres reports it as the first ERC grant in psychology for a Romanian institution.

1 September 2026 · Agerpres

Wild sturgeon, watched in real time on the lower Danube

A monitoring network along about 1,500 km of the lower Danube is in place, with tagged fish and a lab for genetic checks. The point is to know the wild stock well enough that any later talk of fishing has a scientific floor.

1 September 2026 · Romania Insider

A national workshop for AI models has started

Romania began implementing the RO AI Factory, hosted at the informatics institute in Bucharest, meant to be usable by the end of 2027. Universities, labs, and smaller firms are the intended users. It is a start of a building, not a finished computer.

Ferrero says it will build a factory here

The Italian group behind Kinder and Nutella said it will raise a new plant in Romania. Nexta published that confirmation on 8 September. A plan, not a ribbon-cutting.

7 September 2026 · Actmedia

Factories had their best month in two years

The BCR manufacturing index rose to 51.1 in August, from 50.1 in July. Above 50 means expansion. The same wires say this is a turn at a low level, not a recovery already finished.
